# verdicts Specification

## Purpose

The page of one national vote: its result, the majority of voters and the majority of cantons
side by side, and the cantons behind the second, with whatever the sources say about the result.

A vote's page is written when the site is built. Its result is calculated by the
`result-calculation` capability over all entries of the vote's file
(`app/static/data/votes/<id>.json`); its title, kind, official result, reference and note come
from the list of votes (`app/static/data/votes.json`). Both are data files of the `vote-data`
capability: the BFS vote-day files and the Federal Chancellery's validated results, on the list
of municipalities of 2026-01-01, regenerated by the pipeline's update command
(`pipeline/README.md`). The figures in the scenarios are those of
`investigations/data-sources.md`. Dates and numbers are written as the `vote-list` capability
says.

## Requirements

### Requirement: A page for every vote

The build SHALL write a page at `/votes/<id>` for every vote of the list of votes. The page SHALL
show the vote's German title as its heading, its date, its kind and which majorities that kind
needs: "Braucht das Volksmehr und das Ständemehr." or "Braucht nur das Volksmehr.". A tie-break
question, which the calculation does not judge, shows neither; its page says what the
requirement "The tie-break question" asks for instead.

#### Scenario: The page of a vote

- **WHEN** a visitor opens `/votes/5960`
- **THEN** the page's heading is "Volksinitiative «Für Ehe und Familie - gegen die
  Heiratsstrafe»", and it shows "28. Februar 2016", "Volksinitiative" and "Braucht das Volksmehr
  und das Ständemehr."

#### Scenario: A page for each of the votes

- **GIVEN** the data files of 2026-11-14
- **WHEN** the site is built
- **THEN** it holds 125 pages of votes, one for each id of the list of votes

### Requirement: The page holds its result

A vote's page SHALL hold the result calculated when the site is built; the browser SHALL NOT load
the vote's entries to show it. When a vote of the list has no file of entries, or the
calculation refuses one of its entries, the build SHALL fail and name the vote.

#### Scenario: The entries stay out of the browser

- **WHEN** a visitor opens `/votes/5960` and waits until the page has stopped loading
- **THEN** no request of the browser was for a file under `/data/votes/`

#### Scenario: A vote without its file

- **GIVEN** a list of votes that names a vote for which there is no file of entries
- **WHEN** the site is built
- **THEN** the build fails and names the vote

### Requirement: The figures of the result

For every vote the calculation judges, the page SHALL show the yes votes, the no votes and the
yes share that the calculation gives over all entries of the vote, and the turnout: the ballots
cast divided by the registered voters, both summed over the entries.

#### Scenario: A named vote

- **WHEN** a visitor opens `/votes/5960`
- **THEN** it shows the yes votes "1'609'152", the no votes "1'664'224", the yes share "49.16%"
  and the turnout "63.25%" (`investigations/data-sources.md`, "Reference values": 3,354,019
  ballots cast of 5,302,873 registered voters)

### Requirement: The two verdicts side by side

For every vote the calculation judges, the page SHALL show next to each other the majority of
voters, headed "Volksmehr", with its yes share, and the majority of cantons, headed
"Ständemehr", with its cantonal votes in favour and against. Each SHALL read "Ja" when it is
reached and "Nein" when it is not. Below them the page SHALL show the outcome the calculation
gives: "Angenommen" or "Abgelehnt".

#### Scenario: The cantons in favour, the voters against

- **WHEN** a visitor opens `/votes/5960`
- **THEN** the Volksmehr reads "Nein" with "49.16%", the Ständemehr reads "Ja" with "16½ : 6½",
  and the outcome is "Abgelehnt" (`investigations/data-sources.md`, "Reference values")

#### Scenario: The voters in favour, the cantons against

- **WHEN** a visitor opens `/votes/5670`
- **THEN** the Volksmehr reads "Ja" with "54.35%", the Ständemehr reads "Nein" with "10 : 13", and
  the outcome is "Abgelehnt" (`investigations/data-sources.md`, "Reference values")

### Requirement: A majority that is not needed

For a vote that needs only the majority of voters, the Ständemehr SHALL read "nicht nötig" and
SHALL show, greyed out, the cantonal votes the calculation gives; the outcome SHALL follow the
majority of voters alone.

#### Scenario: An optional referendum the cantons were against

- **WHEN** a visitor opens `/votes/6790`
- **THEN** it shows "Braucht nur das Volksmehr.", the Volksmehr reads "Ja" with "50.39%", the
  Ständemehr reads "nicht nötig" with "7½ : 15½" greyed out, and the outcome is "Angenommen"
  (`investigations/data-sources.md`, "Reference values")

### Requirement: The table of the cantons

For every vote the calculation judges, the page SHALL show a table with one row per canton that
has an entry, in the order of the Federal Constitution: the canton's abbreviation, its yes share,
its cantonal votes ("1" or "½") and the side they count for ("Ja" or "Nein"). A canton without
valid votes SHALL show "–" as its yes share. For a vote that needs only the majority of voters,
the table SHALL be shown as well.

#### Scenario: The cantons of a named vote

- **WHEN** a visitor opens `/votes/5960`
- **THEN** the table has 26 rows from ZH to JU; the row of ZH reads "ZH", "43.46%", "1" and
  "Nein" (`investigations/data-sources.md`, "Reference data for the calculation"); the rows of
  OW, NW, BS, BL, AR and AI show "½"; and the cantonal votes of the rows that read "Ja" add up to
  16½

### Requirement: The tie-break question

For a tie-break question the page SHALL show, from the vote's result in the list of votes, the
votes for the initiative (its yes votes) and for the counter-proposal (its no votes), each with
its share of the two, and the cantons for each side. It SHALL say that a tie-break question
counts only when the initiative and the counter-proposal both pass, and that it is not judged
here. It SHALL show neither verdicts nor an outcome nor a table of the cantons.

#### Scenario: The tie-break question of 2026-03-08

- **WHEN** a visitor opens `/votes/6823`
- **THEN** it shows "Für die Initiative: 1'098'341 (37.36%)", "Für den Gegenentwurf: 1'841'208
  (62.64%)" and "Stände: 2 für die Initiative, 21 für den Gegenentwurf", the sentence "Die
  Stichfrage zählt nur, wenn die Initiative und der Gegenentwurf beide angenommen sind; sie wird
  hier nicht beurteilt.", and no "Volksmehr", "Ständemehr", "Angenommen" or "Abgelehnt"
  (`investigations/data-sources.md`, "Output" of `reference_votes.py`)

### Requirement: What the sources say about the result

For a validated vote the page SHALL show the reference to the official publication in German,
as "Quelle: " followed by it. For a vote that is not validated it SHALL show "Vorläufiges
Ergebnis: vom Bundesrat noch nicht erwahrt." When the Federal Chancellery gives a note on the
vote, the page SHALL show it in full, in German, under the heading "Hinweis der Bundeskanzlei".

#### Scenario: A validated vote

- **WHEN** a visitor opens `/votes/5670`
- **THEN** it shows "Quelle: BBl 2013 3129", no note and no mark of a provisional result

#### Scenario: The annulled vote

- **WHEN** a visitor opens `/votes/5960`
- **THEN** it shows "Quelle: BBl 2019 4599" and, under "Hinweis der Bundeskanzlei", the note that
  begins "Das Bundesgericht hat mit Urteilen vom 10. April 2019"

#### Scenario: A result not validated yet

- **GIVEN** the data files of 2026-11-14
- **WHEN** a visitor opens `/votes/6850`
- **THEN** it shows "Vorläufiges Ergebnis: vom Bundesrat noch nicht erwahrt." and no "Quelle: "

### Requirement: Where the municipal figures differ from the validated result

When the yes or the no votes of a validated vote's result differ from the sums of its entries,
the page SHALL still show the calculated figures, and SHALL add, for each count that differs, the
note "Das erwahrte Ergebnis zählt <official> <Ja|Nein>-Stimmen (<reference>); die Zahlen der
Gemeinden, aus denen hier gerechnet wird, ergeben <difference> <mehr|weniger>."

#### Scenario: The vote with the known difference

- **WHEN** a visitor opens `/votes/6650`
- **THEN** it shows the no votes "1'350'525", the outcome "Angenommen", and the note "Das erwahrte
  Ergebnis zählt 1'350'325 Nein-Stimmen (BBl 2024 996); die Zahlen der Gemeinden, aus denen hier
  gerechnet wird, ergeben 200 mehr." (`investigations/data-sources.md`, "Answers", 5)

#### Scenario: A vote without a difference

- **WHEN** a visitor opens `/votes/5960`
- **THEN** it shows no note on the validated result
